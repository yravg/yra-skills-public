# Measure, don't eyeball

**When:** you are about to say a screen, feature or port is good or done.

Evidence beats impression: scan score, contrast ratio, test output, golden mismatches, a render you viewed.

Rules:
- Render at 1440, 1024 and 390 px, keyboard only, dark mode. A passing script is not a view.
- Rust: 0 golden mismatches and the full cargo gate. A SKIP is not a pass.
- Quote the numbers before and after.
