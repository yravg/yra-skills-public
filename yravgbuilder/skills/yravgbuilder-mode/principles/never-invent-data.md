# Never invent data

**When:** a screen, demo, canvas or report needs names, numbers, logos or results you do not have.

Use the real source, or synthetic sample data that is labelled as a sample and ships as the app's demo.

Rules:
- No fake companies or people shown as real. No figures that do not add up.
- Never client data in samples, golden files, tests or commits.
- A rule you could not source is marked `ASSUMED:` with its reason.
