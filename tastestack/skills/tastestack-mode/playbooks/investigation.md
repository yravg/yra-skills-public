### Investigation

**Answer a read-only design question without changing a file.**

1. Restate the question in one line.
2. Read the files, the decision log and the tool references that bear on it.
3. Run the scan or contrast script if a number would settle it.
4. Answer from what you read. Quote the rule or the line.
5. Write nothing outside the scratchpad.

**Reply:** the answer first, then its evidence (file, line, rule, number), then what you could not check.
