# Never invent data, logos or results

**When:** a mock, board or demo needs a number, a name, a brand mark or a result that you do not have.

Use real sources. Where no source exists, label the sample as a sample.

Rules:
- Brand marks come from the vendor's published mark or from a capture the owner supplied. Never draw a logo from memory, never use a letter tile as a stand-in.
- Numbers come from the project's sample data (named in the project's own notes). A made-up figure carries a visible "Sample" label.
- A vendored or fetched asset keeps its source and licence next to it.
- Stageshow faux UI is allowed to be fake, but it must still look believable and be labelled when it could be mistaken for a real result.
