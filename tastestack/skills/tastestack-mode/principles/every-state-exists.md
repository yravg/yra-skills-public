# Every state exists

**When:** you design, audit or fix a screen.

A screen is not done until empty, loading, error, partial, success and disabled are drawn or deliberately ruled out.

Rules:
- List the states from the story or the brief first, then check each against the render.
- Never delete a state to make a board look cleaner.
- Error text says what happened and what to do next, in plain words.
- Keyboard focus and reduced motion count as states.
